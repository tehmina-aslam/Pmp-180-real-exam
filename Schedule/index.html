How to approach Schedule traps: Calculate the longest path, not the shortest-looking branch. Use float before assuming a project delay. Choose crashing when extra cost/resources can shorten critical work and fast-tracking when activities can safely overlap. In agile, keep the sprint timebox fixed and forecast using demonstrated velocity rather than wishful commitments.
