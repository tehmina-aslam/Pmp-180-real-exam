How this exam works

  • 180 questions in 240 minutes. The clock runs continuously except during breaks, when it pauses.
  • Three sections: 40 / 70 / 70. Questions 1–40, then 41–110, then 111–180. A 10-minute break follows each of the first two sections and the exam clock pauses.
  • Case studies live in Section 1. Question 1 is a standalone item; the case studies follow it. The number of case blocks (one to four) is randomized on every attempt, so expect the section to feel different each time — exactly as candidates report.
  • Sections lock. Once you end a section you cannot return to it — exactly as on the real exam.
  • Question types. Single answer, multiple response, matching, enhanced matching (drag onto a chart), pull-down lists, point-and-click diagrams, ordering, and exhibit-based items with tables and charts. Every drag also works by clicking: click an item, then click where it goes. Click a placed item to lift it back out.
  • Tools. Highlight (J), strike out an option (W), calculator, flag for review, question navigator. No references panel during the exam — the real exam has none. The study references open in the results review.
